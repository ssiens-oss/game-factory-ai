# ECHO//LIFE v43 — Quiet Circuit

Puzzle-led city exploration built on v42. Existing builds remain intact.

Find the glowing relay marker, walk within 100 world units, then press F or tap RESTORE RELAY. The world pauses while you solve a puzzle. Route circuits, switch off light grids, and use ordering clues to reconstruct signals. Hints and resets are free. Each relay grants 100 credits, 24 rounds, 25 health and full shields; every three relays advances a district. Puzzle progress survives leaving the panel during a run. No run persistence across reloads.

Four initial patrols (previously 24), at most six (previously 36), at most one reinforcement every 12 seconds. Enemies engage only within 300 units and stay inactive near the relay. Relay radius 180 is a safe zone. Kills still reward combat but never advance relay objectives; Warden spawning is no longer required. All weapons, touch settings, transparent HUD, upgrades and city visuals remain available.

Controls: WASD/arrows or joystick move; F/touch relay button interact; click/tap puzzle tiles; Space/fire defend; Escape/puzzle back return to city. No puzzle timers or failure penalties.
