# ECHO//LIFE v44 — City of Signals

Continues v43’s puzzle-led exploration, light patrols and transparent HUD. Older versions are unchanged.

- Four puzzle types: rotating circuits, Lights Out, signal ordering, and coupled frequency dials.
- Circuit routes alternate between direct, upper and lower paths. Light grids and clue orders vary deterministically for each relay.
- Free hints, resets and up to 50 undo steps per puzzle. Completed relay rewards cannot be undone or claimed twice.
- Relay routes turn through city blocks; every three restores advance a district. Lantern Market, Glass Gardens, Blue Line and Memory Quarter have journal memories.
- Cached lantern, planter, flower and bench artwork adds street detail. Wet pavement reflections and signal trails guide exploration. Reduced-motion preference freezes decorative signal motion.
- J or NOTES opens the field journal and pauses the world. F or the relay button opens a nearby relay.
- Exploration autosaves locally every five simulation seconds, after puzzle moves, on panel close, and on page exit. CONTINUE appears when a valid save exists. Saves include position, puzzle progress, rewards, upgrades and journal; combat encounters restart after loading. Starting a new run replaces the save. Browser storage must be available for persistence.
- HUD settings, weapons, medkits, upgrades, touch controls and archived v43 remain available.
